Gauss-Markov Assumption Summary

Concise diagnostic summary for the panel aid-allocation models.

AssumptionEvidence in this projectSummaryInference note
Linearity in parametersResiduals-vs-fitted plot is produced for M2 and the dependent variable is log(1 + aid).Specification is linear in coefficients, but functional form still depends on visual inspection.Linear OLS remains a reasonable memo baseline, but nonlinear misspecification cannot be ruled out.
Independent sampling / errorsCountry-years repeat within countries; M3 adds country and year fixed effects with SE clustered by country.IID errors are not plausible in the raw panel.Use the clustered-SE fixed-effects model for the most defensible panel inference.
No perfect multicollinearityVIF check on M2 controls; max VIF = 5.45.Some multicollinearity risk remains in the control set.Coefficients are separately estimable without obvious collinearity failure.
Zero conditional meanNot directly testable; M2 adds observed controls and M3 absorbs country and year effects.This remains a substantive identification assumption rather than something verified statistically.Interpret coefficients as conditional associations, not clean causal effects.
HomoskedasticityBreusch-Pagan results: M1 p = < 0.001; M2 p = < 0.001; M3 p = < 0.001.Homoskedasticity is rejected in at least one panel model.Rely on HC1 or country-clustered SE instead of classical OLS SE.
