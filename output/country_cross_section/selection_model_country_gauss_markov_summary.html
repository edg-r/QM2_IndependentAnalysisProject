Gauss-Markov Assumption Summary

Concise diagnostic summary for the country-level cross-section models.

AssumptionEvidence in this projectSummaryInference note
Linearity in parametersResiduals-vs-fitted plot is produced for the controlled cross-section model and the outcome is log(1 + mean aid).Specification is linear in coefficients, with functional form judged mainly from the residual plot.Linear OLS is a concise baseline, but nonlinear misspecification remains possible.
Independent sampling / errorsEach country enters once after averaging 2013-2021 values, but the sample is still observational rather than randomly assigned.The cross-country sample is observational rather than randomly assigned, so independence is only approximate.Interpret the cross-section as descriptive association across countries.
No perfect multicollinearityVIF check on M2 controls; max VIF = 4.49.No strong multicollinearity signal among included controls.Coefficients are separately estimable without obvious collinearity failure.
Zero conditional meanNot directly testable; the model adds standard macro controls but cannot eliminate all omitted variables.This remains a substantive identification assumption rather than something verified statistically.Treat estimates as conditional associations, not strong causal effects.
HomoskedasticityBreusch-Pagan results: All-country M1 p = 0.319; All-country M2 p = 0.328; Recipient-only M2 p = 0.050.No BP rejection at the 5% level; one specification is borderline.Keeping HC1 robust SE is still the conservative choice.
